Part I in one paragraph

An EUR/USD forward (receive N EUR, pay NK USD at T) collateralised in GBP is discounted with the GBP-collateralised curves. Redoing the collateral replication argument with the collateral in another currency gives the rule: a payoff in currency i collateralised in currency k is discounted at ci + yi − yk, where c is the OIS rate and y the gap between the cash rate and the collateral rate. Writing P(k)i(t,T) for that discount factor:

Vt = N [ Xt P(GBP)EUR(t,T) − K P(GBP)USD(t,T) ],    K* = Xt P(GBP)EUR(t,T) / P(GBP)USD(t,T).

With deterministic collateral spreads the switch factors chain through USD, so the five given curves are enough: P(GBP)USD = dfSOFR · dfSONIA / df[GBP,USD] and P(GBP)EUR = df[EUR,USD] · dfSONIA / df[GBP,USD]. The fair strike collapses to the USD-CSA forward Xt df[EUR,USD]/dfSOFR; GBP enters only through the factor dfSONIA/df[GBP,USD] that rescales the mark-to-market. The write-up has the derivation and the caveats.
